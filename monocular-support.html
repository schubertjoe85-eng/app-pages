Monocular Support

Monocular lets you sketch a house with Apple Pencil, build it in 3D, place it on the real site and render it on your iPhone or iPad.

Frequently asked questions

The site or terrain doesn't load. Check your internet connection; satellite images come from Apple Maps and the terrain from public elevation data, so a site needs a connection the first time it is opened.

AI stills or videos fail. These use your own OpenAI and Runway accounts. Check the API keys in Settings and that your account with that provider has credit.

Renders are slow. On-phone rendering runs on your device; a newer iPad renders faster. Lower the quality for a quick preview.
