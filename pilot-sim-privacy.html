Pilot Real World Sim Privacy Policy Pilot Real World Sim does not require an account and does not collect or store any personal information on a server. - Location: the sim does not use your device's location. The position sent to the services below is your simulated aircraft's position, not yours. - No account or email is collected. Subscriptions are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details. - No user data is sold or shared with third parties, and none is used for advertising or tracking. - Flight plans you open from Pilot stay on your device. As you fly, the app fetches public data directly from these providers for the area around your simulated aircraft: - adsb.lol, for live aircraft positions (ODbL). - aviationweather.gov, for airport details and weather reports (METAR and TAF). - OurAirports, for runway data (public domain). - Mapzen / AWS Open Data terrain tiles, for terrain heights. - EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless, for satellite imagery (contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016). - Pilot's ship relay (AIS positions) and Fintraffic Digitraffic, for live ships and Finnish trains, only if you turn those layers on. - Apple Maps, for the map and 3D city imagery. These requests go directly from your device to each provider and are subject to that provider's own privacy policy.
