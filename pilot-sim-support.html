Pilot Real World Sim Support Fly anywhere on Earth over real terrain and maps, with live air traffic, real weather and a tower that sequences you behind real aircraft. Controls - Yoke or stick: drag down to pull up, sideways to bank. Or use the arrow pad (up = nose up). On a keyboard: arrow keys, space for brakes, + and - for throttle. - Throttle: drag the lever up for power. Flaps: tap UP, 1, 2, 3. - Views: Cockpit, Chase, Track-up and Map (pinch to zoom). - Tower: tap "Ready for departure" at the holding point and wait for "cleared for take-off". Pro subscription - Pilot Real World Sim Pro is a weekly auto-renewing subscription. Manage or cancel it in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Use "Restore purchases" in the app on a new device. Flight plans from Pilot - In Pilot, share a flight plan (.pln / .fpl), a route (.gpx) or a logbook flight (.gpx) to Pilot Sim.
